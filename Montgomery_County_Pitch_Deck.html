Workforce plan

How We Recruit & Retain

Retention is an ongoing operational discipline — not a one-time event at contract start. We invest in proven programs, market-data discipline, and concierge-level support to make Armor a place where clinicians want to stay.

  • Economic Research Institute (ERI) — market-based compensation, benchmarked not guessed; reviewed and adjusted regularly
  • Fidelity Nurse Loan Payment — recurring student loan repayment program that materially improves nurse retention
  • White Glove Treatment — concierge onboarding with a dedicated recruiter, expedited credentialing, and a structured 90-day check-in cadence
  • Tiered Nursing Rates — differentiated pay that rewards experience and certifications, creating a clear path forward without leaving the role
  • Enhanced Health Solutions — our wholly-owned sister agency, built to eliminate dependency on outside staffing agencies; we staff from within, on our terms, at our rates
Bottom line: Montgomery County inherits this retention program from Day 1 — built to keep your clinicians in place.
