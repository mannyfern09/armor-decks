2026 standard changes not yet reflected in the training program

Gender-Affirming Health Care Services (F-08)New standard added in the 2026 edition. No corresponding Armor module or calendar course.
Annual competency assessment (C-02)RNs and LPNs/LVNs now require annual competency assessment. EDU-005 exists; execution is missing at six sites.
Medication administration training (C-05)Now required annually for every staff member who administers or delivers medications, with added topics.
Health staff orientation (C-09)Additional topics were added in 2026. Armor's orientation content should be re-checked against the current list.
Infection prevention (B-02)Expanded to cover negative pressure rooms, outbreak management, hand hygiene and disinfection. The June module predates this.
Clinical preventive services (B-03)Annual health assessments and dental examinations are now required.
Health training for custody staff (C-04)Additional topics added. Relevant where Armor delivers or supports officer training.

Calendar courses with no standard driver

These appear on the 2026 annual calendar but map to no NCCHC standard and no role-track module. They are clinically useful but discretionary — worth confirming they are intentional rather than inherited.
